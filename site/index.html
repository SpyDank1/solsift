What this is

A scanner runs every 5 minutes on GitHub Actions (GitHub's minimum, and it is often slower in practice). It discovers newly launched Solana tokens, pulls market, on-chain safety and social data for each one, scores them 0-100 and publishes the result here. It remembers every score and checks back later to learn which signals actually mattered.

Two feeds

  • Scored feed: tokens that already have real trading, with every check run (on-chain risk report, LP lock, socials, sell route, trend fit). Refreshed by each full scan.
  • Live launches: your browser asks Jupiter for new tokens every 5 seconds and scores them on the spot with the same rules. Those checks that need the scanner have not run yet, so a live score is a first impression. Tokens seconds old are almost always capped as high risk, because that is what they are.

What the score is for

It is a 0-100 ranking of how much a token looks like something that survives versus something built to take your money. It sorts and filters the list, hides likely rugs by default, makes the Buy button ask for confirmation on flagged tokens, and decides what the paper bot "buys". It never buys anything on its own, and it is not a price prediction.

How the score is built

  • Hard gates cap the score no matter what else looks good: live freeze or mint authority, dangerous Token-2022 extensions, liquidity that can be pulled, dust liquidity, one wallet owning the supply, a creator with past rugs, or no sell route.
  • Safety and distribution (35%): authorities, top-10 holder share, developer share, LP lock, bundle and insider clusters, third-party risk flags.
  • Market health (20%): liquidity depth, liquidity relative to market cap, and age (the median rug happens within about 35 minutes of launch).
  • Organic momentum (25%): trade count, buy/sell balance, average trade size, turnover, whether volume persists, holders, and Jupiter's organic score.
  • Social credibility (10%): how old the X account is, follower count and growth rate, whether the account actually mentions the token, Telegram size, website domain age, and whether the same links were reused by other tokens.
  • Trend fit (10%): whether the token belongs to a narrative that is attracting volume right now.

How it learns

At discovery the scanner stores each token's features. One, six and 24 hours later it records what happened. Tokens with a 24h outcome become training data for a small logistic-regression model, tested on the most recent 20% it has not seen. The model's share of the final score grows only as it proves it can beat a coin flip; hard gates always override it.

Honest limits

  • Published studies put pump.fun graduation at roughly 0.5-2% and find about three quarters of DEX-listed tokens rug within 24 hours. A good filter still leaves most picks losing.
  • Scored-feed data is minutes old, and all of it comes from free third-party APIs that can be wrong, late or missing.
  • A sell route existing does not prove a sell will succeed. Check before you size up.
  • The paper bot's fills are idealised. Real slippage on thin pools is worse.
